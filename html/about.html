01HOW IT WORKS

How it works.

Residents walk through candidate designs of their own neighbourhood while their physiological response is recorded. What they feel becomes evidence that can change the project while it is still a proposal.

01Measure

EEG, skin conductance, heart rate variability and eye tracking, all non-invasive and wearable.

02Model

A digital twin of the real neighbourhood, so alternatives are compared on the same ground.

03Experience

The twin is explored at human scale in immersive virtual reality, before construction.

04Co-create

Residents, designers and local authorities decide on the same evidence.

02CONCEPTUAL FRAMEWORK

Four layers.
A connected system.

The infrastructural, digital, cognitive and socio-cultural layers connect material places with human experience and community perspectives.

Infrastructural
Material and spatial fabric.
Digital
VR simulations, digital twins and ecosystems.
Cognitive
Human experience and neurophysiological tools.
Socio-cultural
Community, identity, memory and co-creation.
