01PLACE & CONTEXT

Understanding
the local context.

Kyiv joins Belfast and La Palma in a shared exploration of how human experience can inform urban reconstruction.

Each pilot brings a local perspective to the project’s central question: how can places be rebuilt around the people who use them?

02LOCAL CHALLENGES & OBJECTIVES

People first.
Place in context.

The shared research approach connects lived experience, community participation and digital exploration. Local knowledge helps frame questions about how reconstruction can support more inclusive and sustainable environments.

03COMMUNITY & LOCAL PARTNERS

The people behind the place.

Understanding how people use, experience and remember their surroundings is central to the project. Co-creation brings these perspectives into the development of future scenarios.
